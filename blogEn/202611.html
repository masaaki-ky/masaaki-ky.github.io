11/28/2026

The Energy of the Astral Body Increases


(Practice Method)

(1).First, concentrate the winds and drops in the heart using techniques such as vase breathing ormeditation on the letter 'A'.

(2). Next, continue to stop internal dialogue. A specific sequence is necessary; first gathering the winds and bindu from throughout the body into the indestructible drop at the heart, and then dissolving them into emptiness.

(Out-of-body experience of results)

In the evening, after having gotten plenty of sleep, I lie down and practice the meditation method above. Because sleep pressure is low at this time, the success rate for an out-of-body experience increases dramatically; however, the trade-off is that it often ends in a partial separation without the astral body stabilizing. This time as well, I rose up from a dream state, but my surroundings were dark, with only a few tablets written in kanji coming into view, and my astral body was unstable.
Therefore, I attempted the Tibetan Buddhist practice of Serial Dissolving. This is a practice of visualizing the external world and the astral body as Clear Light and dissolving them into the heart. Its purpose is to re-converge the winds scattered in the external world back into the indestructible drop at the heart, thereby drawing out the true Clear Light. For that reason, in the initial stages, even if the Clear Light does not actually appear, visualization alone is sufficient. In my astral body, while repeatedly chanting "Clear Light" as a mantra, I visualized the external world transforming into Clear Light and being drawn into my heart.
As I did this, my astral body began to stabilize, and my surroundings shifted into the scenery of a dimly lit room. As I kept moving around the room while continuously chanting the mantra, I felt the energy of my astral body increase. However, after a little while, a feeling of "that's enough" arose, and at that exact moment, the experience ended.

(Future Adjustment)
From next time onward, I intend to set a clear goal and try to persist a bit longer.
