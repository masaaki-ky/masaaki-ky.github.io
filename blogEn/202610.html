Concentration Method on the Indestructible Drop Before Sleep

When a person dies or falls asleep, the wind(mind) naturally gathers in the indestructible drop within the heart, drawing close to emptiness. However, because consciousness is usually lost, one fails to recognize the Clear Light. Therefore, before going to sleep, consciously concentrating on the indestructible drop in the heart to recognize the Clear Light is the practice of Clear Light in the Six Yogas of Naropa. I have modified this practice slightly for this attempt.

(Practice Method)

(1).Continuously stop inner dialogue

This is the primary practice of Nagualism (Mexican Sorcery). Doing this creates a natural sensation where the breath (wind) is drawn deep into the heart.

(2).Raise the drop up to the heart through the practice of Tummo
Since total loss of consciousness makes it impossible to recognize the Clear Light, the drop—a concentration of energy—is used to sustain the feeling of pure bliss right up until sleep takes over."

(Out-of-body experience of results)
Attempt 1: Moved through the air toward a wall.
Attempt 2: Floated into the air on the roadside and spent some time observing the surrounding scenery.

What made this out-of-body experience (OBE) different from usual was that it occurred right at the moment of falling into the first sleep of the night. Normally, I get dragged down by sleep, so OBEs rarely happen at this timing.
Furthermore, my consciousness remained clear and uninterrupted before and after the departure.
