The Practice of Tummo and Its Effects

Recently, I have been practicing Tummo, the inner heat practice of the Six Yogas of Naropa.

Meditation Method
1. As a preliminary condition, I decided to practice celibacy and avoid releasing sexual energy.
2.While walking, for example, I focus my attention on the tip of the genitals. When I do so, I experience a sense of bliss, along with a sensation as though amrita were dripping down from the crown of my head. I regard this as the Four Joys of Descent. The amrita eventually accumulates at the tip of the reproductive organ.
3. When meditating while lying down, I continuously focus on the lower abdomen while applying muscular tension and breath pressure. This creates an upward pressure within the central channel, causing the amrita to rise. In fact, I can sustain a sensation of the upward-rising energy at the crown of my head—the mind of great bliss. I regard this as the Four Joys of Ascent. I continue meditating while maintaining this state.
4. Maintaining the mind of great bliss at the crown even during sleep leads to the other practices of the Six Yogas of Naropa, including the practice of Clear Light, Illusory Body, and Dream Yoga.


In the evening, I lie down and meditate while applying pressure to the lower abdomen. When I have had sufficient sleep, complete separation occurs less frequently, but my success rate is higher. This time, too, I did not achieve complete separation, but I repeatedly experienced partial separation for an extended period of time.
Afterward, in a dream, I became aware that I was dreaming and glided through the sky for a while.
